# A1 Opportunities V2 — integration acceptance contract
Status: proposed interfaces; not live endpoints.
A1 Opportunities owns organization/campaign/member identity, consent, referral attribution and commission ledger.
A1 GO owns immutable short-link binding and QR generation. Input: organizationId,campaignId,ambassadorId,idempotencyKey,approvedDestination. Output: linkId,shortUrl,qrPayload,qrAssetRef. QR payload must equal shortUrl. Suspension routes to general campaign without new ambassador attribution; historical rights preserved.
A1 Content owns published template versions and rendering. Input: tenant/campaign/template/version/format, idempotencyKey, shortUrl and qrPayload. Output: artifactId, checksum, MIME, dimensions, duration for motion. Only QR and URL overlays may change approved creative. Formats: 1080x1080,1080x1920,1080x1350. Static PNG/JPG; motion MP4/GIF. Decode final QR before release; verify size and template integrity. Caption and hashtags derived from approved campaign facts and language.
Sharing: native file share where supported; otherwise download and copy caption/link. Explicit selection of connected channels; distinguish share initiated from publication confirmed. OAuth scope and consent required for direct publishing, no social account password collection. WhatsApp personal/group/status sharing via user action; no unsupported auto-send claims.
Service calls require scoped authentication, tenant authorization, durable idempotency, retry/outbox, audit and no secret logging. Independent input/output adapters; no shared database or dependency on live integrations for core administration.
Acceptance: two organizations and two campaigns, two ambassadors, immutable separate links, three exact sizes, approved template unchanged outside slots, decoded QR matches URL, retries do not duplicate resources, suspension protects old attribution, actual paid conversion calculates contract-versioned commission once.
Current evidence: GO v1.py exposes only health; Content has asset decision and video job routes, not verified referral rendering endpoint. No live integration claim. Existing V1 stays unchanged.
